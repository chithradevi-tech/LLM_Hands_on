**LLM (Large Language Model)** is an **AI model trained on a huge amount of text** (books, websites, articles, etc.) to **understand and generate human language**.


> **An LLM is an AI model that learns from massive text data to understand, generate, summarize, translate, and answer questions in human language.**


> **LLM = AI that predicts the next word to generate meaningful text.**

### Example

**Input:** "What is the capital of India?"
**LLM Output:** "New Delhi."

### Examples of LLMs

* ChatGPT
* Gemini
* Claude
* Llama

**Remember:**
**LLM = Large Language Model = AI trained on large text data that predicts the next word to generate human-like text.**
