# Important LLM Research Papers

Research papers are the foundation of modern LLMs. Many interviewers ask about the **most influential papers** and what each introduced.

---

# 1. Attention Is All You Need (2017)

**Authors:** Ashish Vaswani et al. (Google)

**What it introduced:**

* ✅ Transformer Architecture
* ✅ Self-Attention
* ✅ Multi-Head Attention
* ✅ Positional Encoding

**Why it's important:**

This paper replaced RNNs and LSTMs with the **Transformer**, which became the foundation of modern LLMs.

**Interview Question:**

> Which paper introduced the Transformer?

**Answer:**

> *Attention Is All You Need (2017).*

---

# 2. BERT (2018)

**Full Form:**
**Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers**

**Organization:**
Google

**What it introduced:**

* Encoder-Only architecture
* Bidirectional Attention
* Masked Language Modeling (MLM)

**Best for:**

* Text classification
* Sentiment analysis
* Question answering

---

# 3. GPT (2018)

**Full Form:**
**Generative Pre-trained Transformer**

**Organization:**
OpenAI

**What it introduced:**

* Decoder-Only architecture
* Autoregressive text generation

**Best for:**

* Text generation
* Chatbots
* Content creation

---

# 4. GPT-2 (2019)

**Organization:**
OpenAI

**What it introduced:**

* Larger Decoder-Only Transformer
* Strong zero-shot capabilities

**Best for:**

* Text generation
* Story writing
* Summarization

---

# 5. GPT-3 (2020)

**Organization:**
OpenAI

**Parameters:**

* 175 Billion

**What it introduced:**

* Few-shot learning
* Large-scale language modeling

**Best for:**

* General-purpose AI
* Coding
* Writing

---

# 6. T5 (2020)

**Full Form:**
Text-to-Text Transfer Transformer

**Organization:**
Google

**What it introduced:**

Treats every NLP task as a **text-to-text** problem.

Example:

```text
Question:
Translate English to French:

Output:
Bonjour
```

---

# 7. BART (2020)

**Organization:**
Facebook (Meta)

**Architecture:**

Encoder-Decoder

**Best for:**

* Summarization
* Translation
* Text generation

---

# 8. Switch Transformer (2021)

**Organization:**
Google

**What it introduced:**

* Mixture of Experts (MoE)
* Sparse activation

Only a few experts are activated for each token.

---

# 9. InstructGPT (2022)

**Organization:**
OpenAI

**What it introduced:**

* Instruction Tuning
* RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback)

Made LLMs much better at following human instructions.

---

# 10. LLaMA (2023)

**Organization:**
Meta

**What it introduced:**

* High-performing open-weight LLMs
* Efficient training strategies

---

# 11. PaLM (2022)

**Full Form:**
Pathways Language Model

**Organization:**
Google

**What it introduced:**

* Large-scale reasoning
* Multitask learning

---

# 12. Mixtral (2023)

**Organization:**
Mistral AI

**What it introduced:**

* Efficient Mixture of Experts (MoE)
* Strong open-weight performance
